By adding Panda to your Discord server, you agree that you have read and agree to this policy.

This is our "Privacy Policy" which sets out the policy which governs our use of information you provide in connection with the Panda Discord bot. The terms "you" and "your" refer to all individuals or entities with access to Discord servers where Panda is present. The terms "we," "us," "our," refer to Panda and "bot" refers to our Discord bot itself. The terms "user", "member", "channel", "guild" and "server" all refer to those respective Discord features.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes in our Privacy Policy will be effective immediately. By using our bot and/or support server, you consent to the collection, use and transfer of your information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree to this Privacy Policy, please do not use our support server or our bot.

### PRIVACY STATEMENT

We respect the privacy of your information. We provide this explanation about our information practices as a show of our commitment to protect your privacy and in the interests of full transparency. This policy describes the types of information we may collect from you or that you may provide when using Panda or Discord servers where Panda is present and our practices for collecting, using, maintaining, protecting and disclosing that information.

This policy applies to information we collect:

Via Panda:

* When you interact directly with the bot by running a command (for example: `/daily`, `/balance`, `/work`, `/afk`).
* When another member of your guild interacts with Panda while mentioning you (for example: `/ship <user>`, `/hug <user>`, `/slap <user>`, `/pay <user>`).
* When you send a message in a channel that has been set up for Panda channel games (Counting, Boom, Guessing, WordChain)
  * For Counting we store:
    * The channel ID.
    * The guild ID.
    * The current number.
    * The last user ID who counted.
    * Channel settings (chat enabled, consecutive counting allowed, language).
  * For Boom we store:
    * The channel ID.
    * The guild ID.
    * The boom number and current number.
    * The last user ID.
    * Channel settings (chat enabled, consecutive counting allowed, language).
  * For Guessing we store:
    * The channel ID.
    * The guild ID.
    * The target number and attempt count.
    * The last user ID.
    * Channel settings (chat enabled, consecutive counting allowed, language).
  * For WordChain we store:
    * The channel ID.
    * The guild ID.
    * The current word and list of used words.
    * The last user ID.
    * Channel settings (chat enabled, consecutive counting allowed, language).
* When you use the economy system (for example: `/daily`, `/work`, `/work-illegal`, `/deposit`, `/withdraw`, `/pay`, `/coinflip`, `/slot`)
  * For Economy we store:
    * Your user ID.
    * Your cash and bank balance.
    * Your last daily, work and illegal-work timestamps.
* When you use `/afk [reason]`
  * For AFK we store:
    * Your user ID.
    * Your AFK reason (if you provided one).
    * Your language (`tr` / `en`) and AFK start time.
* When Panda joins or leaves a server (automatic server log)
  * We log to a private staff channel only:
    * Server name, server ID, owner ID, member count and server icon.

It does not apply to information collected by:

* us offline or through any other means, including on any other website operated by Panda or any third party; or
* any third party, including through any application or content (including advertising) that may link to or be accessible from the bot (for example, top.gg or other bot listing websites).

### THE INFORMATION WE COLLECT AND HOW WE COLLECT IT

**Personally Identifiable Information**. We only collect personally identifiable information that you provide to us voluntarily through interactions with the bot. This means we do not require you to register or provide information to us in order to view our bot listing. Our bot only gathers personally identifiable data through interactions with the bot. This information is necessary to make our features work and helps us provide a better user experience for all Panda users.

The information we always collect when you use a Panda command is:

* Your [Discord ID](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/206346498-Where-can-I-find-my-User-Server-Message-ID-).
* Your [Discord username](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/12620128861463-New-Usernames-Display-Names).
* The name and ID of the server where you run a command.
* The command you used including any arguments (for example: `/pay @user 100`).
* The date and time at which you used the command.
* Your Discord client locale for automatic language support (`tr` / `en`).

We sometimes also collect:

* User IDs of other users you may have interacted with through a Panda command (for example: `/ship <user>`, `/pay <user>`, `/hug <user>`).
* Public profile data only displayed, never stored, when you use `/user-info` (avatar, banner, badges, roles, join dates).

> To respect your server's privacy, we **NEVER** collect the contents of normal chat messages, only Panda commands and messages sent in dedicated game channels chosen by your admins (Counting / Boom / Guessing / WordChain + AFK mentions).

> Server admins can limit where Panda reads game messages by only starting games in specific channels and by restricting the bot's permissions to those channels. If no game is active in a channel, Panda ignores normal messages there except for AFK mention checks.

The information we collect when you vote for Panda on third-party website Top.gg is:

* Your [Discord ID](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/206346498-Where-can-I-find-my-User-Server-Message-ID-).
* The date and time at which you voted for the bot.

This vote check is done via top.gg API. If top.gg is unreachable, Panda fails open and lets you use the command anyway.

Panda has **no Premium, no payments, no shop**. We never ask for or store card numbers, PayPal emails, or email addresses.

> To protect your identity and your data, we avoid storing personally identifiable information where possible. Game and economy data is keyed by Discord ID only. AFK reasons are stored as you typed them — please do not put passwords, addresses or other sensitive data in your AFK reason, it will be shown to anyone who mentions you. Error logs (private staff channel only) may contain command name, guild/channel/user ID, error message and stack trace for debugging and are kept max 90 days.

**Non-Personally Identifiable Information**. Panda has no website tracker of its own. Through Discord itself, Discord Inc. may automatically collect technical information (IP, device, usage) as described in Discord's [Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy). We do not operate our own cookies, analytics SDKs or ad trackers.

**Information We Collect Through Automatic Data Collection Technologies**. As you use Panda, we automatically keep:

* Details of game sessions to resume games after restarts.
* Economy cooldowns (`lastDaily`, `lastWork`, `lastIllegalWork`) to enforce fair play.
* Shard status, latency, uptime and guild counts (`/statistics` shows these live, it does not store them).
* Server join/leave events for abuse auditing (private log channel only).

We do not track your activities across third-party websites.

### THE WAY WE USE INFORMATION

We use this information to run economy, games, AFK and fun commands correctly, to fulfil purposes you provide it for, to improve our bot, to statistically analyse usage, and with your consent. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

We may also use or disclose information to resolve disputes, investigate problems or enforce our Terms of Use or Discord's [Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms).

### DISCLOSURE OF YOUR INFORMATION

We may disclose aggregated information without restriction (for example: total server count posted to top.gg every 30 minutes).

We may disclose personal information to fulfill the purpose you provide it for, to a successor in merger/transfer, with your consent, to delete your data on request, to comply with court orders, to enforce our Terms or Discord's Terms, or to protect safety / prevent fraud and cheating.

Removing Panda from your server auto-deletes that server's game data. Economy and AFK data is deleted on verified request via support server.

### DATA RETENTION

* Game data for a channel is deleted when the channel is deleted.
* All game data for a guild is deleted when Panda is removed.
* AFK is deleted on your next message or on request.
* Economy balances kept while used, deleted on request.
* Error logs max 90 days, server logs max 12 months.
